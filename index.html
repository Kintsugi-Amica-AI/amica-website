What Amica does

Quiet tools for loud moments.

Every feature is built to be used one-handed, at night, by someone who might be frightened. Nothing flashy, nothing that gives you away.

One-hold SOS with live location

Press and hold for two seconds. Amica gives you five more to cancel, then sends your live location to everyone in your circle.

Emergency

Smart journey timer

Set where you're going and when you should get there. If you don't check in as safe, Amica escalates to your circle automatically.

Your circle

Add the people you trust as guardians. Those who also use Amica get instant push alerts; everyone else gets an SMS.

Bus & train stop alert

Pick the stop you're getting off at. Amica sounds an alarm when you're close, so you can rest your eyes on the long ride home.

Fake call

Schedule a realistic incoming call to step out of an uncomfortable situation. It looks like your phone's own call screen, not a safety app.

Stealth voice SOS

Choose up to ten secret phrases. Say one during a fake call and Amica alerts your circle silently, without you touching the screen.

Scan before you ride

Scan a taxi or tuk-tuk number plate to see how other riders rated it, and send the plate to your circle as you board.

Discreet mode

A dark, low-glare theme with silent, preview-free notifications. A bright screen on a bus at night shouldn't announce a safety app.

How it works

Set up once. Protected every time you leave.

  1. 1

    Create your profile

    Sign up with email or Google, add your phone number and, if you like, medical notes for responders.

  2. 2

    Build your circle

    Choose the guardians Amica should reach first: family, a best friend, a flatmate.

  3. 3

    Start a journey

    Walking, by bus, train or taxi: set your destination and Amica estimates when you should arrive.

  4. 4

    Check in, or let Amica act

    Tap “I'm safe” when you arrive. If you don't, your circle gets your last location and a call is placed to your primary contact.

Inside the app

Soft by design. Serious underneath.

Amica's “Blossom” design uses lavender and orchid for everyday actions. Rose-red is reserved for emergencies, so it can never be mistaken for anything else.

What we promise

Principles we build by.

01

Readable in the dark

Every line of text meets at least a 4.5:1 contrast ratio, in both light and dark mode.

02

Red means emergency

Rose-red appears only on SOS surfaces. Nothing else in the app borrows that colour.

03

Honest about limits

We tell you when a message was sent, not pretend it was delivered. Phones, SIMs and networks can fail, and we say so.

04

Your data, your circle

Your location is shared with the guardians you choose, when you choose. It is never sold or used for ads.

Frequently asked.

Everything you need to know about how Amica keeps you and the people you trust connected.

Getting started

When can I download Amica, and which phones does it work on?

Amica is being built for Android first and is currently in testing. We are finishing the core safety features and trying them on real phones and real journeys before a wider release.

To be one of the first to try it, fill in the contact form below and choose “Early access”. We'll email you when a test version is ready, with simple steps to install it.

How do I get started once I install the app?

Setting up takes about five minutes:

  1. Create your account with your email or Google, and add your phone number.
  2. Build your circle — add at least one trusted person (a parent, sibling, friend or flatmate). One of them becomes your primary contact.
  3. Allow the permissions Amica asks for, so alerts can actually reach people (see “Why does Amica ask for so many permissions?” below).
  4. Optional: record a secret voice phrase and add medical notes for responders.

The home screen shows a “You're protected” check, so you can see at a glance whether anything is still missing.

Does Amica cost anything?

The app is free. There are no ads, and we don't sell your data.

When Amica alerts your circle it uses your phone's normal SMS and calls, so your mobile operator may charge its usual rates for those messages — just like texting someone yourself. Push alerts to guardians who also have Amica use mobile data or Wi-Fi.

Which languages are supported?

Amica is being built in English, Sinhala (සිංහල) and Tamil (தமிழ்), so everyone in your family can use it comfortably. The alert messages your guardians receive are kept short and clear so they can be understood at a glance.

Why does Amica ask for so many permissions?

Each permission is there so help can actually reach you. Amica asks for them when a feature needs them, and you can review them any time in your phone's settings:

  • Location — to share where you are during an SOS or a journey.
  • SMS and phone — to text your circle and call your primary contact.
  • Contacts — to pick guardians quickly from your address book.
  • Notifications — for safety check-ins and alerts from people you guard.
  • Microphone — only for the secret voice phrase during a fake call.
  • Camera — only for scanning a vehicle's number plate.

Safety features

What exactly happens when I press SOS?
  1. Press and hold the SOS button for 2 seconds. A short hold stops it going off in your bag.
  2. You then have 5 seconds to cancel if it was a mistake.
  3. After that, everyone in your circle gets an SMS with your live location. Guardians who also use Amica get an instant push alert as well.
  4. The SOS screen shows you who has been contacted, so you're never left guessing.

When you're safe, tap “I'm safe now” and your circle is told that too.

How does the journey timer work?

Before you set off — walking, by bus, train or taxi — choose where you're going. Amica estimates your arrival time from the distance and a typical travel speed, and you can adjust it.

If you don't check in as safe by then, Amica asks whether you're okay. If there's no answer after one minute, your circle is sent an SMS with your last known location. After three minutes, Amica also tries to call your primary contact.

Running late but fine? Add more time with one tap. Your circle can also follow your journey from a private link while it's active.

Do my guardians need to install Amica?

No. Anyone with a mobile phone can be in your circle — they receive your alerts as normal SMS messages with a map link to your location.

Guardians who do install Amica get extra benefits: instant push alerts, a live view of your journey, and a way to let you know they've seen your alert and are responding.

What is the bus and train stop alert?

Pick the stop you're getting off at before you board. Amica sounds an alarm when you're close — 2 km before your stop by default, and you can change the distance.

The route is worked out once while you have signal, so the alarm still works if your connection drops along the way. It's ideal for long evening rides when you're tired or don't know the route well.

What are the fake call and the secret voice phrase?

Fake call lets you schedule a realistic incoming call, so you have a polite reason to step away from an uncomfortable situation. It deliberately looks like your phone's own call screen, not a safety app.

Secret voice phrase: you choose up to ten ordinary-sounding phrases, such as “Did you feed the cat?”. If you say one during a fake call, Amica silently alerts your circle — no need to touch the screen or let anyone see you asking for help.

What is “Scan before you ride”?

This feature is coming soon. Before getting into a taxi or tuk-tuk, you'll be able to scan its number plate to see how other riders have rated it, and send the plate number to your circle as you board.

After the ride you can leave a 1–5 star rating with an optional comment to help the next rider. Ratings are shared experiences, not official checks of the driver.

Will Amica work without internet or signal?

Partly — and we'd rather be clear about that:

  • SMS alerts only need normal mobile signal, not mobile data.
  • The stop alert keeps working offline once your route has been set.
  • Push alerts and live journey links need an internet connection.
  • With no signal at all, messages can't be sent until your phone reconnects.

Some phones also stop apps running in the background to save battery. We recommend allowing Amica to run in the background and testing it once with a friend.

Privacy & data

Who can see my location?

Only the guardians you choose, and only when you share it — during an SOS, or while a journey you started is active. Amica doesn't track you in the background the rest of the time.

Journey links sent to your circle only show what they need (your first name, position and estimated arrival) and stop working when the journey ends. The Amica team never sells your data or uses it for advertising.

Can I use Amica discreetly?

Yes. Discreet mode switches to a dark, low-glare screen and keeps notifications silent with no message preview, so a bright safety app doesn't draw attention on a bus at night.

The fake call screen looks like a normal phone call, and the secret voice phrase lets you ask for help without touching your phone at all.

Is Amica a replacement for the police or an ambulance?

No. Amica helps the people you trust find you quickly, but it doesn't contact emergency services for you. If you are in immediate danger, call them first:

  • Police Emergency: 119
  • Suwa Seriya ambulance: 1990

Phones, SIM cards and networks can fail, so it's always worth having a backup plan with the people close to you.

Yes, we'd love to hear from you. We're especially interested in working with:

  • Workplaces — support for staff on late shifts and commutes.
  • Transport operators — safer taxi, tuk-tuk and bus journeys.
  • Women's organisations and NGOs — feedback, awareness and outreach.
